El Cacique Agüeybaná Battle Bot

Designer · Team Captain

Designed and built a 7 lb combat robot through a three-month development cycle. As team captain, I coordinated the transition from SolidWorks concepts to fabricated components and final assembly while managing structural requirements, competition constraints, and a strict weight limit.

  • Developed the mechanical architecture and component layout in SolidWorks.
  • Coordinated fabrication tasks, team responsibilities, and build progress.
  • Integrated the structure, drive components, weapon system, and protective panels for competition.
SolidWorksMechanical DesignFabricationAssemblyLeadership

02A // DESIGN

Architecture within a strict weight limit

The design phase established the robot’s overall layout, protective structure, weapon geometry, and component packaging. SolidWorks was used to balance structural support, serviceability, and competition performance without exceeding the 7 lb class limit.

02B // MANUFACTURING

Turning the CAD package into hardware

During manufacturing, the team converted the selected design into the robot’s structural panels and mechanical components. I coordinated responsibilities and build sequencing so fabrication could progress within the short competition schedule.

02C // ASSEMBLY

Mechanical integration and final configuration

The final phase brought the structural shell, internal hardware, belt-driven weapon system, and protective panels together as one competition-ready assembly. Multiple views document the finished packaging and mechanical interfaces.
